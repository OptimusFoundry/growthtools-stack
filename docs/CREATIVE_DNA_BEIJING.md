# Creative DNA: Beijing & China

Visual style reference for AI video generation. Profiles of top creative agencies and commercial directors operating in the Beijing/Shanghai/China market.

## Part 1: 20 Agencies & Studios

### 1. The Nine Shanghai

- **Type:** Independent Creative Agency ("Commercial Art Lab")
- **Notable Clients:** Coca-Cola, Durex, Volvo, Tencent, Alibaba Cloud, Yotkan Ancient City, Dynasty Daming Palace, Beijing 2022 Winter Olympics
- **Signature Style DNA:** Blends fine art with commercial storytelling. Campaigns feel like gallery installations that happen to sell products. Heavy emphasis on cultural narrative, merging Chinese heritage with contemporary design and technology.
- **Color Tendencies:** Rich, saturated palettes drawn from traditional Chinese painting — deep vermillion, ink black, jade green, gold leaf. Contrast between muted earth tones and sudden bursts of festival color.
- **Typography Style:** Calligraphic Chinese characters treated as art objects. Custom brush-stroke titling mixed with clean modern sans-serif for bilingual layouts.
- **Motion Language:** Theatrical reveals, slow transformations, objects morphing between cultural symbols. Physical installations captured cinematically.
- **Pacing:** Deliberate and contemplative with sudden dramatic climaxes. Long establishing shots followed by rapid montage sequences.
- **Signature Techniques:** Blending performance art, immersive installations, and film into single campaigns. Technology-augmented cultural storytelling (AR, projection mapping, interactive digital). Cross-disciplinary — a campaign might be a song, a play, or a performance piece rather than a traditional ad.
- **Best For:** Cultural brand films, heritage luxury campaigns, festival celebration content, tourism and destination videos, art-meets-commerce executions.

### 2. TBWA\Media Arts Lab Shanghai

- **Type:** Dedicated Apple Agency (Full-Service Creative)
- **Notable Clients:** Apple (exclusive — "Shot on iPhone" campaigns, Chinese New Year films, product launches)
- **Signature Style DNA:** Cinematic storytelling that feels like indie film, not advertising. Annual Chinese New Year short films are cultural events in China. Human-centered narratives that demonstrate product capability through authentic emotion rather than feature lists.
- **Color Tendencies:** Naturalistic, film-grade color science. Warm skin tones, atmospheric golden hour, desaturated backgrounds that let subjects glow. Seasonal palettes — warm ambers for CNY, cool blues for tech.
- **Typography Style:** Apple's San Francisco typeface with generous whitespace. Minimal text overlays. Product names in clean white against dark backgrounds. Bilingual Simplified Chinese set in PingFang.
- **Motion Language:** Smooth, controlled camera movements — Steadicam walks, slow dolly pushes, gimbal tracking shots. Cinematic depth of field showcasing iPhone camera capabilities.
- **Pacing:** Long-form narrative pacing (10-16 minute films). Patient scene-setting, emotional build, cathartic resolution. No rush — trust the story.
- **Signature Techniques:** "Shot on iPhone" constraint as creative catalyst. Collaboration with world-class film directors (Marc Webb, Michael Gracey, Peng Fei, Bai Xue). Stories rooted in specific Chinese cultural moments — family reunion, generational conflict, identity.
- **Best For:** Premium brand films, product-as-hero narratives, long-form emotional storytelling, cinematic demo content.

## Part 3: Beijing/China Style Summary

### What Makes Chinese Advertising Unique

**Digital-First by Default.** China leapfrogged traditional advertising. With 700 million+ daily active users on Douyin alone, the Chinese ad industry is built around mobile-first, social-native, e-commerce-connected content. WeChat, Douyin, Xiaohongshu (RED), Bilibili, and Tmall/JD.com each require platform-specific creative. There is no single "TV spot" equivalent — campaigns must be natively designed for 5+ platforms simultaneously.

**KOL Culture and Livestream Commerce.** Over 47% of Douyin users have purchased directly through influencer livestreams. The line between content, commerce, and advertising is deliberately blurred. KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders) are not endorsers — they are co-creators and distribution channels. Livestream commerce generated over 3.5 trillion yuan GMV on Douyin alone in 2024. Advertising must work within this ecosystem, not fight against it.

**Festival Campaigns as Cultural Events.** Chinese New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival, Singles Day (11.11), Double 12, and 618 are not just seasonal campaigns — they are cultural tentpole moments where brands compete for national attention. Apple's annual "Shot on iPhone" CNY film is a cultural event. Brands release feature-length content, limited editions, and immersive experiences. Red and gold are not cliches — they are culturally required visual languages that must be deployed with sophistication.

**Guochao (National Trend) Movement.** Approximately 80% of Gen Z consumers actively engage with guochao trends. Chinese consumers increasingly prefer products with Chinese cultural identity — traditional design elements, Chinese mythology, calligraphic typography, and heritage craftsmanship reimagined with contemporary aesthetics. Foreign brands must navigate this cultural confidence carefully.

**Color Symbolism is Non-Negotiable.** Red conveys luck, happiness, and festivity. Gold signifies wealth and prosperity. Yellow represents imperial authority. White is associated with mourning, not purity. These are not optional brand guidelines — they are deeply embedded cultural codes that inform every visual decision.

### The Douyin/TikTok Influence on Chinese Video Ad Style

Douyin has fundamentally reshaped Chinese advertising aesthetics:

- **2-Second Hook Rule:** Content must grab attention in the first 2 seconds or users scroll past. This has compressed storytelling into front-loaded emotional or visual hooks.
- **"Real Vlog" Aesthetic:** The dominant trend is less polished, more emotional content with an "I'm just like you" tone. Skincare, food, lifestyle, and fashion brands use this deliberately unpolished feel.
- **Vertical-First Composition:** 9:16 format is default. Compositions, typography, and motion design are all designed for vertical viewing.
- **Music and Audio as Primary Driver:** Trending Douyin music and sound effects drive editing rhythm. Audio hooks are as important as visual hooks.
- **Fast Pacing with Emotional Cues:** Quick transitions, text overlays, trending effects, and rapid demonstrations. But the most viral content combines fast pacing with genuine emotional moments.
- **Merchant Livestream Shift:** 70% of Douyin e-commerce livestreams are now merchant-led (rather than independent KOL-led), meaning brands need in-house content creation capability, not just influencer partnerships.
- **Platform-Specific Content:** What works on Douyin does not work on Xiaohongshu (which favors aesthetic, aspirational layouts) or Bilibili (which favors long-form, personality-driven content). Chinese video advertising is inherently multi-format.

### The Chinese Creative DNA in 5 Bullet Points

1. **Cultural Heritage as Creative Fuel:** The most awarded Chinese work draws from thousands of years of visual culture — calligraphy, ink painting, martial arts choreography, mythology, festival traditions, and imperial aesthetics — reinterpreted through contemporary technology and global production standards. Chinese pride is the emotional engine.

2. **Digital Ecosystem Fluency:** Chinese creatives think in ecosystems, not channels. A campaign is simultaneously a WeChat Moment, a Douyin challenge, a Tmall storefront, a Xiaohongshu post, and a livestream event. Content is designed to flow natively across platforms, with each version optimized for its context.

3. **Spectacle Meets Intimacy:** From Zhang Yimou's Olympic ceremonies to Apple's 16-minute CNY short films, the best Chinese advertising oscillates between massive cultural spectacle and quiet personal intimacy. Scale and tenderness coexist — a campaign about one billion people watching the same moment can also be about one family sitting together.

4. **Commerce and Creativity are Inseparable:** In China, every piece of content is a potential point of sale. Unlike Western advertising that often separates "brand" from "performance," Chinese creative seamlessly integrates storytelling with commerce. The buy button is never far from the emotion.

5. **Speed and Reinvention:** Chinese advertising moves faster than any other market. Trends cycle in weeks, not months. Agencies like Banana Balloon launch and win global awards within 18 months. An agency ranked first one year may not exist the next. The Chinese creative DNA is defined by relentless forward motion, constant experimentation, and the belief that the next campaign should make the last one obsolete.
